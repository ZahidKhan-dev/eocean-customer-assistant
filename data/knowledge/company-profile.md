## 1. Company Overview

Eocean (E Ocean Private Limited) is a Pakistan-based communications technology company. It provides Communications Platform as a Service (CPaaS) solutions that help businesses communicate with customers through digital channels.

Its communication solutions include SMS, WhatsApp, voice and other messaging channels.

Website: https://eocean.net/

## 2. Communication Channels

### SMS API
Enables businesses to send SMS communications, including alerts, one-time passwords (OTPs), notifications and campaigns.

### WhatsApp Business API
Supports business conversations on WhatsApp, including customer support, marketing, notifications and automated interactions.

Official information:
https://eocean.net/whatsapp-business-api/

### Voice API
Supports programmable voice communication and voice-related business workflows.

### RCS
A richer messaging channel included in Eocean's published communication portfolio.

Exact availability and features must be confirmed with Eocean.

## 3. Core Service Categories

- Customer messaging: promotional and transactional messaging, alerts and notifications.
- Customer interaction: two-way messaging, voice communication and customer-support workflows.
- Identity and verification: OTP and two-factor authentication solutions.
- Campaign automation: targeted communication and scheduled campaigns.
- Enterprise integration: connecting communication services with business applications and workflows through APIs and portals.

## 10. Customer Support Rules

The assistant must:

1. Answer using approved company information.
2. Never invent prices, discounts or commercial commitments.
3. Never invent API specifications or technical instructions.
4. Never promise an SLA, uptime or support response time without verified information.
5. Ask the customer to contact sales for pricing and proposals.
6. Escalate account-specific, billing, security and unresolved technical issues to a human representative.
7. Clearly explain when information is unavailable.
8. Never reveal credentials, private customer information or internal documents.

## 11. Information Requiring Confirmation

Do not give definitive answers about:

- Current pricing and taxes.
- API endpoints, authentication and payloads.
- Webhook specifications and rate limits.
- SLAs and support hours.
- Country and operator coverage.
- Current product-level feature specifications.
- Current partnership status and contractual commitments.
- Customer-specific account or delivery information.

These details must be confirmed through current authorized Eocean sources.
